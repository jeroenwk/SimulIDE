# Model limits

What SimulIDE's parts leave out, met while building book circuits with the
create-circuit skill (issues of `jeroenwk/create-circuit`). SimulIDE favours fast,
simple models: a limit is fixed when a book circuit depends on it, and kept when the
stand-in gives the circuit the same result. Each issue says which, and why: kept ones
are closed (not planned) with the reason, the stand-in and when to revisit; the rows
marked **fix planned** are open issues with a create-circuit TODO task (label
`task:...`). Until a fix lands, say so in the circuit's description, next to the result
it changes, and build the closest stand-in given here.

## Simulation step

| Limit | Stand-in / what to do | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| reactive parts integrate with a fixed step (`reaStep` 1 µs by default, backward Euler): an LC tank at 50 kHz (19 steps a period) decays numerically, an RC of 1 µs reads as a staircase with half the peak; an oscillator's amplitude decays at ω²·reaStep/2 per second whatever the op amps' gain (a 1 kHz two-integrator loop: 20 /s at 1 µs, 5 /s at 250 ns), which reads like a hidden op-amp pole; a bootstrapped twin-T notch (Scherz fig 9.22) loses its null as the op-amp gain rises (2 kHz residual at Gain 1e5: 0.40 V p-p of 2 V at 1 µs, 0.058 V at 100 ns, 0.034 V at 20 ns) | at least 100 steps per period or time constant: lower the circuit's step (`stepSize` / `reaStep`) or the capacitor's `ReaStep`; check by halving it | [#276](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/276), [#320](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/320), [#513](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/513), [#437](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/437) |
