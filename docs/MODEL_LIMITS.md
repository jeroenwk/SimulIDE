# Model limits

What SimulIDE's parts leave out, met while building book circuits with the
create-circuit skill (issues of `jeroenwk/create-circuit`). SimulIDE favours fast,
simple models: a limit is fixed when a book circuit depends on it, and kept when the
stand-in gives the circuit the same result. Each issue says which, and why: kept ones
are closed (not planned) with the reason, the stand-in and when to revisit; the rows
marked **fix planned** are open issues with a create-circuit TODO task (label
`task:...`). Until a fix lands, say so in the circuit's description, next to the result
it changes, and build the closest stand-in given here.

## Semiconductors

| Part | Limit | Stand-in / what to do | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| BJT | defaults: `IS` 1e-13 A gives VBE 0.596 V at 1 mA (books assume 0.65–0.7 V); `VA` 0 = no Early effect (infinite r_o: a current-mirror load rests saturated, current sources are perfectly flat) | set `IS` for the datasheet's VBE (1e-14 A: 0.655 V at 1 mA) and `VA` for r_o = (VA + VCE) / IC (small-signal parts: 50–150 V) whenever a gain, a mirror or a current source depends on it (fixed: SimulIDE PR #73), or pick the part's `Model` (docs/BJT_MODELS.md), which sets IS, gain and VA | [#70](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/70), [#289](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/289), [#290](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/290), [#291](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/291), [#304](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/304) |
| BJT | `Cje` = `Cjc` = 0 pF by default: no Miller capacitance, so no high-frequency roll-off | set `Cje` / `Cjc` from the datasheet when a bandwidth is the point, or pick the part's `Model` (sets the junction capacitances; diffusion capacitance TF is still left out) | [#208](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/208) |
| BJT, Diode, JFET | few named models (no 1N4001–4004, 1N5818, SD101, 2N3053, BFY51, BF862; the BJT has 42 models since its `Model` property, docs/BJT_MODELS.md) | the nearest model of the family (1N4007; a power Schottky: 1N5819, 1 A, not the 15 mA signal 1N5711), the nearest BJT `Model` or a Custom BJT with `Gain`, a JFET with `Model=Custom` and I_DSS / V_p from the datasheet's mid-spread | [#239](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/239), [#288](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/288), [#292](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/292), [#300](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/300), [#309](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/309) |
| JFET | Shichman-Hodges square law, but `Lambda` multiplies the ohmic region too (1 + λ·V_DS), and the gate junctions leak: the 0.5·V_DS gate-feedback linearization (a JFET as a voltage-controlled resistor) leaves a residual that grows with the swing and near pinch-off (r+ / r− split 5–7 % at ±0.4 V) [INFERRED from docs/JFET.md] | `Lambda=0` when the linearity is the point; give the residual and the book's claim | [#389](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/389) |
| JFET | editing any value turns `Model` into Custom (by design, docs/JFET.md); a named model is one sample of the datasheet's spread (2N5484 `Vto` −1.5 V puts a −2 V gate past pinch-off) | `Model=Custom` with I_DSS / V_p from the datasheet, the part named in the description; check a named model's `Vto` against the circuit's gate voltages | [#417](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/417) |
| Mosfet | `RDSon` is the on-resistance at V_GS = 10 V: at other drives R = RDSon × (10 − Vth) / (V_GS − Vth) | for a book's R_ON at V_GS, set RDSon = R_ON × (V_GS − Vth) / (10 − Vth) | [#281](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/281) |
| Mosfet | three pins: no body pin, no body diode | a diode across drain-source when the circuit can forward-bias it | [#212](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/212) |
| BJT | no Darlington (TIP102/TIP107, TIP120): a single BJT with `Gain` 1000 has the gain but one V_BE drop (0.65–0.75 V), not two (1.4–2.5 V), so an emitter follower sits 0.7 V too high | two BJTs wired as the Darlington (Q1 emitter to Q2 base, collectors together), with the part's internal base-emitter resistors when the datasheet shows them (TIP102: about 8 kΩ and 120 Ω) | [#489](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/489), [#491](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/491) |
| Zener (Custom) | soft knee: a 1.24 V part holds about 1.17 V at 0.4 mA | read the actual voltage; give the difference | [#118](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/118) |
| Zener | no shunt reference (LM385, LM336, TL431): a Zener (Custom) of 2.5 V reads 2.37–2.40 V at 20–100 µA, where the LM385 holds 2.500 V from 20 µA to 1 mA | a Zener (Custom) and the shortfall named next to the result, or a Battery of the reference voltage (`Resistance` ≈ the datasheet's dynamic impedance, about 1 Ω) when the exact voltage matters (it then also sources current) | [#432](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/432) |
| Led | keeps its full `Threshold` drop at near-zero current (a real LED drops 1.2–1.5 V at 0.1 µA) | an off-state node reads lower than on the bench | [#168](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/168) |
| Seven segment | each segment is a 2.4 V threshold + 1 Ω, not a diode | currents differ from a book's LED estimate | [#201](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/201) |
| PUT | `Cj1`..`Cj3` default to 0 pF: firing is one solver step, the capacitor's charge vanishes in the step instead of flowing out through the cathode resistor, so there is no cathode pulse | set the junction capacitances from the datasheet when the pulse is the point; check the period on the anode | [#286](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/286) |
| Triac, Diac | the gate trigger is a current into the model: the gate node stays near 0 V, the Diac reads 0 A while it fires | check the firing angle instead of the gate pulse | [#247](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/247) |
| all | no temperature: no thermal drift or runaway | show the electrical analogue (vary the bias part) | [#112](https://github.com/jeroenwk/create-circuit/issues/112) |
