# LM2575 buck regulator

A native part (`src/gui/circuitwidget/components/active/lm2575.{h,cpp}`), Active / Other
Active, library name `LM2575` ("Buck Regulator"). Pins `vin`, `onoff`, `gnd` (left), `out`,
`fb` (right). `Model`: ADJ, 3.3, 5.0 (default), 12, 15.

## Model

- **Switch** from VIN to OUTPUT: 0.9 Ω on (VSAT 0.9 V at 1 A [DOC]), 100 MΩ off. The external
  catch diode, inductor and output capacitor make the converter, as in the datasheet.
- **Oscillator** 52 kHz [DOC]: the switch turns on at the start of each cycle (unless the
  duty cycle is below 2 %: pulse skipping at light load) and off after the duty cycle, at
  most 98 % [DOC].
- **Feedback**: the FB pin is compared with 1.23 V [DOC]. Fixed models scale FB by 1.23 V /
  Vout (their internal divider): connect FB to the output. ADJ: an external divider, Vout =
  1.23 V × (1 + R2/R1).
- **Error amplifier and compensation** [INFERRED]: once per cycle, a PID on the feedback
  error, normalised by the plant gain (VIN × 1.23 / Vout): integral at 2π × 100 Hz,
  proportional 0.5, derivative 0.4 ms. The derivative damps the output LC, which the real
  part leaves to the output capacitor's ESR; SimulIDE's capacitors have none by default.
  Tuned for the datasheet's 330 µH / 330 µF; it settles in about 15 ms from power-up.
- **Current limit** cycle by cycle at 2.8 A typ [DOC]: the switch turns off for the rest of
  the cycle (CLI `read`: `limited` counts those cycles).
- **ON/OFF**: above 1.4 V (VIH 2.2 V min) the switch stays off and the supply current is
  50 µA [DOC]; otherwise 5 mA [DOC]. Below VIN 4.75 V it does not switch.

Set `reactStep` to about 1 % of the period (200 ns) so the inductor and capacitors follow
the switching.

output ripple from the capacitor's ESR (an ideal capacitor gives 1.7 mV instead of tens of
mV: give the capacitor a series resistance to see it), the switch's real VSAT curve.
