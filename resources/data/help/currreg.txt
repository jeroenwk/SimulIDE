Current Source IC: adjustable 3-terminal current source, e.g. LM334, LM234, LM134.

A true floating 2-terminal current source (current in at V+, out at V-) set by one resistor
from R to V-: the part holds 64 mV across it, so I_SET = 67.7 mV / R_SET at 25 °C (the bias
current, I_SET/18, included). Works from about 1 V up to 40 V across it; below that the
current falls off (LOW V state). The current is proportional to absolute temperature, so it
also works as a temperature sensor. More in docs/CURRENT_SOURCE_IC.md.

Property: (default value)

- Model: (LM334)
   LM334, LM234, LM134 or Custom. Changing a parameter makes it Custom.

- Junction Temperature: (25 °C)
   The sense voltage scales with absolute temperature.

- Sense Voltage: (64 mV)
   Voltage held across the set resistor at 25 °C.

- I_SET / I_BIAS: (18)
   Typical ratio of the total current to the bias current; editing it replaces the model's
   ratio curve with this constant.
