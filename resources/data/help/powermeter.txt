Power meter with output pin.

Put it in the lead of a source whose other terminal is ground, the red pin
toward the source (+) and the black pin toward the circuit. It shows:
- the power that the red side delivers to the black side: the red pin's
  voltage to ground times the current into the red pin. Now, or the mean over
  Avg_Time (property Reading).
- the r.m.s. voltage at the red pin and the r.m.s. current, over Avg_Time.
- the power factor: the mean power divided by (V r.m.s. x I r.m.s.).
  1.000 for a battery or a resistor; less when voltage and current are out of
  phase or the current is distorted (a capacitor, a rectifier with a capacitor).
The voltage is taken against ground: in the ground lead it reads zero.
With the source on the black pin the power and the power factor read negative.

Pins:
- Red Pin: toward the source (+).
- Black Pin: toward the circuit.
- Right Pin: Output reading as voltage (1 V = 1 W).
