Programmable unijunction transistor (PUT), e.g. 2N6027.

An anode-gate thyristor: it fires when the anode rises about a diode drop above the gate,
and stays on until the anode current falls below the valley current. The gate voltage,
set by an outside divider (R1, R2), "programs" the peak voltage, and the divider's
resistance (RG = R1 || R2) the peak and valley currents. Used in relaxation oscillators,
ramp generators and SCR triggers.

Modelled as the two-transistor latch of the datasheet (a PNP and an NPN coupled, with a
shunt on the NPN base). More in docs/UNIJUNCTION.md.

Property: (default value)

- Model: (2N6027)
   2N6027, 2N6028 or Custom. Changing a parameter below makes it Custom.

- PNP gain: (100)

- NPN gain: (10)
   Sets the valley (holding) current.

- NPN base shunt: (150 kΩ)
   Sets the peak (trigger) current.

- Junction capacitances J1, J2, J3: (0 = none)
   Anode-gate, centre and gate-cathode junctions; typical values are used while noise is on.
