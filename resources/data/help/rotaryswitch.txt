Rotary switch: 1 to 4 poles, 3 to 12 positions, 30° per detent.

A round body with the knob in the middle. The commons (A, B, C, D) are the pins on the left; the throws (A1, A2, ..., B1, ...) are on the right, the top and the bottom. In position n, each common is connected to its throw n: A to A2, B to B2, ... while the simulation runs.

Use it: drag the knob, click it (one detent clockwise, Shift + click the other way), turn the mouse wheel over it, press Shift + its Key, or set Position.

Property: (default value)

- Poles: (1)
   Number of commons. Poles x Positions is at most 12, as on the SR25 / SR26 deck.

- Positions: (4)
   Number of detents, 3 to 12.

- Position: (1)
   Where the knob stands, 1 to Positions.

- Shorting: (no)
   Yes: make before break. While it changes position the next throw connects first, the other one opens after Overlap Time.
   No: break before make. The pole touches nothing for Transit Time.

- Key: ()
   Capital letter. Press Shift + this letter while the simulation runs to turn the switch one detent clockwise.

- Contact Resistance: (50 mOhm)
   Of a closed contact.

- Transit Time: (0 ms)
   Not shorting only: how long the pole touches nothing.

- Overlap Time: (1 ms)
   Shorting only: how long both throws are connected.

- Bounce Time: (0 ms)
   Each contact chatters for this long when it makes (not shorting) or breaks (shorting).

- Bounces: (5)
   Changes of the chatter.
