Bipolar Junction Transistor.

Property: (default value)

- PNP: (yes)
   PNP or NPN.

- Gain: (100)
   Current gain.
    
- Threshold: (0.670736 V)
   Base-Emitter diode threshold.

- C-E Breakdown Voltage: (0 V = none)
   BVceo: past it, avalanche current from collector to emitter (state BREAKDOWN).

- E-B Breakdown Voltage: (0 V = none)
   BVebo: a base driven this far below the emitter (above it for PNP) conducts
   to the emitter, as a zener (5 mA at BVebo; small-signal parts: about 5-6 V).
